Mexico City, 1971. Lieutenant María and the Azules investigate a student activist's murder — uncovering a conspiracy reaching back to the Halconazo massacre. Created by Fernando Rovzar, produced by Lemon Studios. 8 episodes through September 30, 2026.

Historical Context — Season 2

Set in June 1971, centered on the Halconazo (Corpus Christi Massacre) ↗, where paramilitary "Halcones" attacked student protesters. Also draws on the 1968 Tlatelolco massacre.

Apple TV+ Significance

Las Azules was Apple TV+'s first original production in Latin America. Available in Mexico, Spain, Italy, South Africa, Austria, and many more markets.
