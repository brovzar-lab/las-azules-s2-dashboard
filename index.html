Historical Context — Season 2

Set in June 1971, centered on the Halconazo (Corpus Christi Massacre) ↗, where paramilitary "Halcones" attacked student protesters in Mexico City. The season also draws on the 1968 Tlatelolco student massacre.

Apple TV+ Significance

Las Azules was Apple TV+'s first original production in Latin America. Its continued success validates Apple's Spanish-language content strategy. Available in Mexico, Spain, Italy, South Africa, Austria, and many more markets.
